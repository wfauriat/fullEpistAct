Where the ideas in Agir d'après une représentation du monde come from

Each idea in the 2026 monograph is dated to its earliest written trace and classed by who wrote that trace: you alone, you working with an LLM, or an LLM working under your direction.

Human · certain

The 2022 book: no LLM whatsoever, in the thinking or the writing.

Human · LLM era

Written by you, with no LLM writing, after your claude.ai chats had begun on 7 Nov 2025, so the thinking may carry their influence. Covers the talk (guaranteed free of LLM writing), the Nov 2025 notes, the Dec 2025 seminar, and your instructions during the build.

Mixed

Your ideas shaped in LLM dialogue (your L3h, L3 and L4): chat transcripts, your own words after the dialogues began, or LLM prose under your editorial control, as in the arXiv article and the essays.

LLM

First written by a model working from its own reading of your material: Failing Differently, the 12 Nov lineage analysis, and the specs and chapters of the build. Includes the literature the models brought in.

L0–L4 is the provenance scale from your Nov 2026 housekeeping archive: L0 hand-written, L1 mostly or all AI, L2 AI-assisted training, L3 AI proposal with heavy human editing (L3h when the ideas and formulations are mostly yours), L4 transcript or summary of a human–AI conversation. These labels are coarse and were assigned after the fact, so they are used here as one signal among others (who wrote the text, dates, character counts), never as proof on their own.

Limits of this map

  • Only the 2022 book is certain. You guarantee that it involved no LLM at all, and that no LLM wrote any of the talk. Everything you wrote after 7 Nov 2025, the date of your first claude.ai conversation, may carry the influence of those chats, even where you wrote every word. Many ideas the talk states also appear in the 2022 book (possible worlds, the shipowners, the turkey, roulette), so they count as certain. The four marked "LLM era" have no 2022 trace.
  • Most Dec 2025 – May 2026 dialogues are gone. 85 % of the claude.ai conversations were deleted after harvest, and only their metadata survives. Everything marked Mixed may hold ideas that are wholly yours, or ideas a model proposed and you kept. Your own account is that the ideas and formulations were mostly yours (L3h), and the character counts above fit that only partly: you typed about a tenth of the text.
  • Git dates are entry dates. They show when a phrase was written into a repository, not when it was first thought.
  • Some texts were used only through their provenance records: Precisely Wrong, The Perspectival Trap, the November essay and the October letter. No message in the claude.ai export was read.
  • Three classifications rest mainly on your L-labels. The bridge essay (L3) and the November essay (L3h) are marked Mixed, and the 16 Dec seminar (L0) Human, because of how you labelled them. The texts themselves were drafted or partly written by a model. Three ledger rows depend on the bridge essay's label: the stack of contracts, failure addresses and the jagged frontier, and the case studies.
  • The ledger is a selection of 46 ideas. Its counts show where ideas came from, not how much each one weighs in the book.
